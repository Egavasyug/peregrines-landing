How adoption works

Source: MESSAGING.md — t_ai2_gtm_claim_surface_honesty_001

Users adopt agentic AI in the flow of real work — progressive coaching on the first useful job — not via Soft Soft PDF-first courses.

  • First useful win — connect your corpus, run one worthwhile job, see a real result.
  • KM OJT — knowledge compounds on the job path; coaching rides along.
  • Governed control plane — CP orchestrates; you stay in the seat (HITL where it matters).
  • Side-rail optional — reference help exists; Adequacy is the finished useful job.
  • Honest roadmap — incomplete limbs (e.g. tenant RAG self-serve) stay roadmap until kn PASS.

FAQ / Footer Honesty
